## Understand

The dynamic behavior of the tank is described by a volume balance:

$$
A\frac{dh}{dt}=q_{in}-q_{out}
$$

with gravity-driven outlet flow:

$$
q_{out}=C_dA_o\sqrt{2gh}
$$

This example uses the current LaSiCoP tank baseline parameters: 2 m tank diameter, 0.1 m outlet diameter, 3 m maximum level, $C_d=0.62$, $\Delta P_v=10$ psi, and $C_{v,max}=300$.

> **Purpose of this demo:** to show how one entry can combine explanations, equations, interactive controls, and plots. The components can still be refined before becoming the final learning activity.
