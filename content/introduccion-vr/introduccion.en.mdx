## Welcome to the VR environment

This first experience is designed to become familiar with the virtual laboratory before starting the chemical engineering activities.

We can explore the lobby and campus, identify the main spaces, and practice basic interaction.

## Basic controls

During the tour we can practice:

- movement inside the environment;
- orientation and observation;
- object selection;
- interaction with panels and laboratory elements.

## Campus exploration

The route can also include optional points with brief information about LaSiCoP, project development, and the spaces represented in the virtual environment.

### Before entering a simulation

The goal is to learn navigation and interaction first, so the student can focus on the process when entering an activity such as the level tank.
